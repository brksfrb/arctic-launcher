## What's new

### Arctic Client (in game)
- **Motion blur** and **item physics** (dropped items lie flat and tumble as they fall), on 26.1–26.3 and 1.8.9–1.12.2. Both are in Mods → Visual.
- **1.8.9–1.12.2 catch up:** the minimap, duels and switching accounts in game.
- **3D cosmetics on every version** (1.8.9 to 26.3), with a soft shine on sculpted pieces. New: 20 sculpted cosmetics, 2 HD animated capes, 50 skins and an arms slot.
- **HUD:** new widgets take your HUD style; Arrows and Held show just the item; the Compass pointer color can be changed; World time and Entities have proper icons.
- **Servers** that ask whether you use Arctic or Polarium (like BotPvP) now get an answer.
- **Offline accounts** can be added from the in-game Account page.
- Looks (skins, capes, cosmetics, emotes) are now chosen in the launcher only; the emote wheel stays in game.

### Launcher
- **Windows search** finds the launcher: it adds itself to the Start menu.
- **Feedback:** suggest a feature or report a bug from the launcher, with cleaned-up logs if you like.
- **Instances can keep their own settings** instead of sharing them with your other instances.
- **Modpacks:** a Modpacks banner on the Instances page, drag-and-drop `.mrpack` install, and `.zip` packs with a Modrinth index install too.
- **Discover → Partners:** servers hosted with Flash Hosting.
- **Import** lets you pick exactly what to bring in from another launcher.
- **Styles:** Classic is a black theme now, and the new Vanilla style keeps Minecraft's own menus.

### Faster starts
The game reaches the title screen several seconds sooner: only the Fabric API parts in use are loaded, classes are loaded ahead on other cores, the game's own classes aren't re-verified, files aren't re-checked on every start, and the loading screen fades out faster.

### Fixed
- Copying screenshots to the clipboard works again.
- 26.3 no longer crashes at startup, and joining servers no longer fails with a "Network Protocol Error".
- Each launcher page keeps its own scroll position.
- Polarium copied for the wrong Minecraft generation no longer stops the game from starting.
