## What's new

0.6.9 fixes a crash that stopped Minecraft 1.21.6 to 1.21.10 from launching, and lets you rebind the Arctic menu key. Right Shift stays the default.

### In game
- The Arctic menu key can be rebound in the Arctic menu under Client, or in Minecraft's Controls screen under Arctic. Esc restores Right Shift, and in-game hints show the bound key.
- The Arctic button sits inside the pause menu.
- Wheel scrolling is faster in 1.8.9 and 1.12.2 menus.
- Title and pre-world menus on older versions are smoother.
- Chat history kept on disk comes back after vanilla clears the chat.
- Cosmetics 3D preview capes render at native resolution, and GIF capes play in the preview.

### Launcher
- The default instance is now called Arctic, not Vanilla.
- Arctic no longer installs PolyPatcher or OneConfig on Forge 1.8.9. A Patcher or OneConfig you added yourself is left alone.
- You get a restart notice if mods change while the game is running.
- Interrupted downloads resume, flaky ones are retried, and errors name the failing host or the right loader.
- The malware check now warns only on known malware, and remembers a dismissed warning by the JAR's hash.
- Browse cards put the select checkbox on the right.

### Fixed
- Minecraft 1.21.6 to 1.21.10 no longer crash at launch because of the badge code.
- Rejected Minecraft sessions are refreshed before reconnecting.
- Clearer diagnoses for missing Forge language providers, OpenGL frame-wait crashes, and unclear exit 77.
