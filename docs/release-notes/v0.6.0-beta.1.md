## What's new

This beta collects everything added since 0.5.0, mostly in the Arctic Client. The version ranges say where each feature works.

### Arctic Client: new
- **Lock Slots**: Alt + click locks a slot (a padlock shows on it; clicks and the drop key are blocked there). Modern versions and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Inventory Search**: Ctrl + F in a chest or your inventory finds items by name. Modern versions and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Shulker Peek**: hold Shift over a shulker box for what is inside, Shift + Alt for every slot. The window takes the box's colour, and chests, barrels, hoppers and other containers that carry items work too. Minecraft 1.11 and newer.
- **Command Palette** (Ctrl + K): search mods, servers and friends, and jump into Quick Join game modes.
- **Combat Feedback**: a hit marker, hit and kill sounds and a low-health warning.
- **Saturation Bar** now works like AppleSkin: a golden overlay on the hunger icons your saturation covers (a whole icon for 2 points, half for 1).
- **No Hurt Shake**: the view stops tilting and shaking when you take damage. 1.14 to 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Dynamic FPS**: a lower frame rate limit while the game is in another window, idle or in a menu. 1.14 to 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Particle Control**, **Held Item** (where your hand and its item sit in first person) and **Portal Screens** (the inventory, chat and menus stay open in a Nether portal).
- **New HUD widgets**: CPU, PC CPU, PC RAM, allocation rate, GPU, VRAM, frame time and 1% low; Block, Fall and Pearl; a floating TNT timer. Every widget can hide while Tab (the player list) is held.
- **Tab Ping** shows the number where the game's ping bars are (1.16 to 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2). An **Arctic badge** shows above heads of Arctic players (1.16 and newer).
- **OptiFine capes** without OptiFine: players who have an OptiFine cape and no Arctic one show theirs (Mods, OptiFine Capes).
- **One Arctic button** opens a hub: Configure HUD, Mods, Replays, Friends, Presets, Accounts and Menu Style, with a badge for unread chat and new features. The Mods menu has categories (Combat, Inventory, Movement, Tools, Players), HUD widget groups, NEW marks, presets and distinct icons.
- **Server list in game**: hover a row and its icon becomes a play arrow, and clicking the icon joins at once. Disconnect screens stay the game's own when you chose the game's server list.
- **Debug Screen** can hide groups of F3 lines (version, FPS, coordinates, light, entities, system, targeted block and more), has its own text size, and its colours work on 1.21.9 and newer.
- **Emoji button** in the chat, and pop-ups on the connecting and loading screens.
- **Fonts**: the smooth font is pixel-sharp by default (Menu Style, "Smooth edges" brings the soft edges back), with softer shadows under it. Fancy works with Patcher's font renderer on 1.8.9.
- **Shoulder pets** blink.

### Launcher: new
- **Change an instance's mod loader**: another build of the same loader, or Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge or Forge on the same Minecraft version. Its mods are looked up first, and the ones without a version there are turned off, not deleted.
- **Duplicate instance**: a copy with its own folder, the same mods, packs, settings and configs (worlds when you ask).
- **Send logs to staff**: a button in the Logs tab sends the log you are looking at (user name, folders, e-mail and IP addresses and tokens taken out) and gives you a short code to paste in your ticket.
- **A global "Play with Arctic Client" switch** in Settings, set in setup too.
- **Setup** can add a desktop shortcut (on by default) and asks, once and optionally, how you heard about Arctic. Only your answer is sent.
- **Crash reports** can carry a contact (a Discord name or e-mail) and read the game's latest.log and Java's crash file when the game dies quietly. Java's crash file is trimmed first: no command line, VM arguments or environment variables.
- **Import** HUD layouts from Fast Client and Silent Client.
- **Settings in groups** and a sidebar in sections. The instance page says when the client is off for everything. Play times over a day read as days. The mod download bar sits above the search field with a percentage.
- **Mac**: the app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a warning. Homebrew works: `brew install brksfrb/arctic/arctic-launcher`.

### Fixed
- The 1.8.9 to 1.12.2 mouse pointer comes back on open screens (a grabbed mouse or an empty cursor, seen in fullscreen).
- Glow-ink sign text no longer flickers with the smooth font (1.21.9 to 1.21.11).
- The Mods menu's category chips wrap instead of running past the panel.
- In the Logs tab the mouse wheel and dragging past the edge scroll while you select. Friends' screenshots and chat pictures are decoded with size limits, and chat pictures no longer show empty.
- The page's scroll bar sits on the window's edge.
