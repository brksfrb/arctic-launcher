## What's new

### Fixed
- **The launcher no longer keeps resizing itself** on screens with display scaling (125%, for example): Auto interface size measured the screen again right after changing size, and flipped between two sizes. This update installs by itself.
- **Cosmetics previews**: items sit in the middle of their cards, faces seen at a slant (a brim's underside as you tilt) no longer vanish early, and cubes inside each other stop flickering.
- **The character preview** keeps its size while it turns.

### Cosmetics
- **Skin gallery is easy to find**: Skins, Skin gallery and Capes sit together under Looks, and the gallery opens first when you have no skins yet.
- **Your own capes stay**: every cape you add keeps its card on the Capes page, so you can switch between them in one click (right-click one to remove it).
- **Adding a skin or a cape** is a card at the front of its list.
- **Zoom further** in the character preview (0.4× to 4×); zooming in moves toward your face.
- **Shadow Brim**: a new black wide-brim hat (Head).

### Download

Windows: `arctic-launcher-windows-x64.exe` · macOS: `arctic-launcher-macos.zip` · Linux: `arctic-launcher-linux-x64`. The Windows files are signed (Open Source Developer Leyla Barlak, Certum).
