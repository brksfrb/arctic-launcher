## What's new

0.6.7 starts faster, checks mods and game files more carefully, and adds a few in-game tools. Badges stay sharp when you zoom, and 1.8.9 replay can record and render properly again.

### Faster launches
- First install, repeat launches and version switches are quicker. Loader, client and performance-mod work now run while vanilla files are checked.
- Play uses the Fabric loader already known from a previous start. The launcher refreshes that in the background when it opens, and a newer loader is used on the next start.
- Downloads that slow to a crawl near the end are recovered, and the progress bar stays accurate.

### Security and files
- Enabled mods are checked for known or obvious malware before launch (on by default). You can turn the mod off, cancel, or continue. This cannot guarantee a mod is safe.
- Official Windows builds are signed. The launcher warns you if your copy isn't signed by us, or if the signature is invalid.
- Damaged cached game JARs are spotted from their ZIP headers and replaced before launch. Your own mods are left alone.
- On older Windows, the bundled UCRT libraries are kept when the system ones aren't enough.

### In game
- Enchant descriptions add a short explanation under each enchantment.
- Armor hider can hide armor on your own player, with small checkboxes in the armor slots.
- Hide your own potion particles, or other players', as two separate settings.
- A toggle to ignore Mojang's server blocklist (on by default), including on 1.8.9.
- Name-tag and TAB badges stay sharp and correctly placed when you zoom.
- Skin cards have a menu and a PNG download. If you have a premium account, you can also set your Arctic skin as your Minecraft skin.
- Chat timestamps line up in a fixed column.
- On 1.8.9, HUD widgets drag more smoothly.

### Replay
- On 1.8.9, you can record a replay while you're already in a world.
- Rendering a replay no longer gets stuck on "Downloading terrain" (this also fixes the same hang on 26.3's loading screen).

### Launcher
- Discover uses a Category dropdown, shows shaders and resource packs as image cards, and marks installed content.
- Replies to reports you sent now show in the launcher.
- Installed versions still launch if Mojang's version list can't be reached.
- The launcher download is smaller than in 0.6.6.

### Fixed
- Clearer crash messages for a full disk, a JEI recipe-loading freeze, damaged JARs, missing Windows DLLs, and several other cases (custom Java on older versions, Sodium driver blocks, graphics driver hangs, More Chat History, and more).
- Animated capes keep the frame rate you set.
- Fixed a hotbar-scroll clash with Perspektive.
- The last good 3D cosmetics catalog is kept if a refresh fails.
