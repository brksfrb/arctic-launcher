## What's new

### Fixed
- **Server list pings finish**: servers no longer sit at "Pinging…" after you come back from a server or open Discover, and Multiplayer no longer crashes while the pings come in.
- **Smooth text without dark edges** on 1.17 to 1.21.11 (it was already fixed on 26.1 and newer).
- **Boss bar** has its icon in the mods menu, and the Scoreboard and Boss bar settings show a preview even while the real ones are on screen.
- **Continuity and Sodium**: a mod that only needs a newer version of another now offers **Update** first, instead of saying they can't run together.
- **Play together**: the invite code wraps inside its card in a narrow window.
- **Theme and client style tiles** stay inside their rounded borders.
- **Discover servers**: Sort has its own row, so Surprise me no longer covers it.
- **A game that fails before any mod loads** (a part of the mod loader that can't be read) starts next time with its files checked and without Arctic's start-up speed-ups, and its crash report says how Java was started.

### New
- **Big modpacks get more memory by themselves** when their instance has no memory setting of its own (80+ mods 5 GB, 150+ 6 GB, 250+ 8 GB, never more than your PC can spare).
- **Trash** on the Instances page: removed instances are listed there, and you can bring one back in one click or delete it for good.
- **Modpack and mod search** keep loading as you scroll down (they stopped at the first 20).
- **Share** looks up mods you put in by hand on Modrinth, so mods copied from another folder are shared too.
- **Menu Style** is a button next to Edit HUD in the Arctic Client's mods menu.

### Cosmetics
- **Void Brim**: the Shadow Brim hat in pure black (Head).
