## What's new

0.6.0 collects everything added since 0.5.x, mostly in the Arctic Client, and runs on every version from 1.8.9 to 26.3. The version ranges say where each feature works.

### Arctic Client: new
- **Pickup Display**: a feed of the items you pick up, stacked by name (1.8.9 to 26.3).
- **Attack Range Indicator**: the crosshair brackets turn when a target is within reach (1.8.9 to 26.3).
- **Client Sort**: press R over a chest or your inventory to sort it, with a key setting (1.8.9 to 1.12.2 and 1.14 to 26.3).
- **Auto LAN**: while Arctic Launcher hosts in Play together, your singleplayer world opens to LAN by itself (survival, no cheats; a switch in the mod list).
- **Dynamic FOV** can ignore speed (sprint, potions, flying) and a drawn bow, each on its own, on every version.
- **Arctic watermark** is a small snowflake in the corner you choose (size, opacity, optional version, optional ARCTIC text).
- **Held Item** size goes up to 500%. Menu lists scroll smoothly.
- **Polarium** (the entity renderer) now also ships for 1.8.9 and installs with the Performance switch; for 26.2 and 26.3 the bundled build is the fixed one.
- **Lock Slots**: Alt + click locks a slot (a padlock shows on it; clicks and the drop key are blocked there). Modern versions and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Inventory Search**: Ctrl + F in a chest or your inventory finds items by name. Modern versions and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Shulker Peek**: hold Shift over a shulker box for what is inside, Shift + Alt for every slot. The window takes the box's colour, and chests, barrels, hoppers and other containers that carry items work too. Minecraft 1.11 and newer.
- **Command Palette** (Ctrl + K): search mods, servers and friends, and jump into Quick Join game modes.
- **Combat Feedback**: a hit marker, hit and kill sounds and a low-health warning.
- **Saturation Bar** now works like AppleSkin: a golden overlay on the hunger icons your saturation covers (a whole icon for 2 points, half for 1).
- **No Hurt Shake**: the view stops tilting and shaking when you take damage. 1.14 to 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Dynamic FPS**: a lower frame rate limit while the game is in another window, idle or in a menu. 1.14 to 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Particle Control**, **Held Item** (where your hand and its item sit in first person) and **Portal Screens** (the inventory, chat and menus stay open in a Nether portal).
- **New HUD widgets**: CPU, PC CPU, PC RAM, allocation rate, GPU, VRAM, frame time and 1% low; Block, Fall and Pearl; a floating TNT timer. Every widget can hide while Tab (the player list) is held.
- **Tab Ping** shows the number where the game's ping bars are (1.16 to 1.21.11 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2). An **Arctic badge** marks Arctic players: above heads from 1.16, and as a real picture in the player list and on name tags on 1.8.9 to 1.12.2. Settings choose where it shows and whether it marks you too.
- **OptiFine capes** without OptiFine: players who have an OptiFine cape and no Arctic one show theirs (Mods, OptiFine Capes).
- **One Arctic button** opens a hub: Configure HUD, Mods, Replays, Friends, Presets, Accounts and Menu Style, with a badge for unread chat and new features. The Mods menu has categories (Combat, Inventory, Movement, Tools, Players), HUD widget groups, NEW marks, presets and distinct icons.
- **Server list in game**: hover a row and its icon becomes a play arrow, and clicking the icon joins at once. Disconnect screens stay the game's own when you chose the game's server list.
- **Debug Screen** can hide groups of F3 lines (version, FPS, coordinates, light, entities, system, targeted block and more), has its own text size, and its colours work on 1.21.9 and newer.
- **Emoji button** in the chat, and pop-ups on the connecting and loading screens.
- **Fonts**: the smooth font is pixel-sharp by default (Menu Style, "Smooth edges" brings the soft edges back), with softer shadows under it. Fancy works with Patcher's font renderer on 1.8.9.
- **Shoulder pets** blink.
- **Motion blur, redone**: it smears along the picture's movement from the camera's turn (and a stretch when you move forward), with no ghost copies. Pick a mode (turning and moving, turning only, moving only) and a strength slider. Hand and item blur too.
- **1.7 Animations** (1.8.9): the sword swings while you block, swing while eating, drinking and drawing a bow, the 1.7 bow stretch, smooth sneaking, and the 1.7 item position in first and third person.
- **Chat**: click a line in the history to copy it; **Chat Mute** (players and words, also gone from history and search); timestamp options (12-hour, AM/PM, seconds, brackets, bold, italic); keep the chat when you leave a server; stacking options (time frame, ignore blank lines and dividers, also on 1.8.9 to 1.12.2); a Hide chat toggle; **Auto Text** answers a chat line by itself (when it says X, send Y).
- **Quickplay**: join Bed Wars, SkyWars, Duels and other minigames with a click, and play the last one again.
- **Particle Control per kind** (smoke, flames, explosions, crits, portal, water, potion, redstone, lava, block breaking).
- **Visual and view**: Damage Tint (the screen flushes in a colour you pick when you are hit), Snaplook (hold a key to see yourself from the front), Hide Titles, Fog (push it away or remove it), Glint Colorizer (items and armor), Mob Size (every creature but players bigger or smaller; 1.13 to 26.3 and 1.8.9 to 1.12.2), Chunk Borders, Shiny Pots (every potion glints), Team View (teammates outlined through walls, a box on 1.8.9) and 2D Items (dropped items are flat sprites that face you), all on every version, and a Sound Changer that quiets or silences single sounds by a piece of their name.
- **Widgets**: Cooldowns (pearl, chorus, wind charge, shield; 1.9 to 26.3), Horse Stats (speed and jump of the horse you ride or aim at; every version), a Looking at widget (the block or creature under your crosshair), and the Items widget counts the items you choose.
- **Scrollable Tooltips**: a tooltip taller than the screen moves with the mouse wheel.
- **Any colour** on every swatch row (a picker with hex entry), and a **Rescan** button for controllers on 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- Hitboxes and Chunk Borders switch on and off at once on 1.21.11 to 26.3 (they used to need a rejoin).

### Launcher: new
- **Change an instance's mod loader**: another build of the same loader, or Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge or Forge on the same Minecraft version. Its mods are looked up first, and the ones without a version there are turned off, not deleted.
- **Duplicate instance**: a copy with its own folder, the same mods, packs, settings and configs (worlds when you ask).
- **Send logs to staff**: a button in the Logs tab sends the log you are looking at (user name, folders, e-mail and IP addresses and tokens taken out) and gives you a short code to paste in your ticket.
- **A global "Play with Arctic Client" switch** in Settings, set in setup too.
- **Setup** can add a desktop shortcut (on by default) and asks, once and optionally, how you heard about Arctic. Only your answer is sent.
- **Crash reports** can carry a contact (a Discord name or e-mail) and read the game's latest.log and Java's crash file when the game dies quietly. Java's crash file is trimmed first: no command line, VM arguments or environment variables.
- **Import** HUD layouts from Fast Client and Silent Client.
- **Settings in groups** and a sidebar in sections. The instance page says when the client is off for everything. Play times over a day read as days. The mod download bar sits above the search field with a percentage.
- **Mac**: the app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a warning. Homebrew works: `brew install brksfrb/arctic/arctic-launcher`.
- **OptiFine capes of your accounts** in Cosmetics, Capes: one click wears one as an Arctic cape, so Arctic players see it with or without OptiFine.
- **Pick the modpack version**: every modpack in the browser has a Versions button listing its versions with their Minecraft version and loader.
- **Names the system can't find**: when Windows can't look up a website (error "No such host is known"), the launcher asks Cloudflare over HTTPS instead. It is not used behind a proxy; set `ARCTIC_NO_DOH=1` to turn it off.
- **Import from SKlauncher**: 3.x profiles (with their memory setting, even for versions not downloaded yet) and 4.x instances, including ones in a moved data folder. Accounts are never read.
- **Look up an OptiFine cape by name**, even when the launcher has no account of that name.
- **Link your Discord** to your Arctic profile, which also restores an offline account on a new PC.

### Fixed
- **Play together finds your world on more PCs**: the launcher listens for the game's "Open to LAN" announcement on every network adapter (VPN, Hamachi, WSL, virtual adapters), and falls back to the port in the game's log, checked with a Minecraft status ping. Announcements only count when they come from your own PC.
- **1.8.9 to 1.12.2 emotes** no longer leave players' legs drifting away afterwards, and 1.8.9 no longer throws errors or shifts your armor when the server sends your own equipment.
- **Fullbright** no longer leaves its gamma in `options.txt` on 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- **Crash fixes**: the smooth font when its settings or the GUI scale change (26.x), a render pass left open on 26.3, the 1.13.2 title screen, the 1.8.9 clouds with Polarium, and the game starting from a Flatpak document-portal folder.
- **Chat** lines no longer flash black as they fade out, and Dynamic FPS no longer caps the game while you use the HUD editor.
- **Accounts**: the account page pages its list instead of overflowing, and only the account of the right kind shows as playing. The game's account switch asks only for Microsoft accounts and needs a yes in the launcher; the question has "Yes, always" to ask once per account, and the game waits for your answer instead of failing and asking you to try again.
- **Game logs** keep the last five runs of each instance (`game-<name>.1.log` and up) instead of being overwritten by the next launch.
- **Hardening**: the session file is private to your user and removed once read, long command lines go through an argument file, name lookups ask several resolvers at once, and settings and `arctic.json` are saved with backups.
- The 1.8.9 to 1.12.2 mouse pointer comes back on open screens (a grabbed mouse or an empty cursor, seen in fullscreen).
- Glow-ink sign text no longer flickers with the smooth font (1.21.9 to 1.21.11).
- The Mods menu's category chips wrap instead of running past the panel.
- In the Logs tab the mouse wheel and dragging past the edge scroll while you select. Friends' screenshots and chat pictures are decoded with size limits, and chat pictures no longer show empty.
- The page's scroll bar sits on the window's edge.
- The game no longer fails to start with a damaged `arctic-mod.jar`: it is checked before every start and written again when it is broken.
- Arctic's HUD widgets no longer disappear while hitboxes are on (1.21.11 to 26.3).
- A controller stick that kept the player walking after it was let go is released.

### Also new since the betas
- **Connected glass**: glass and panes connect into one clean sheet, with an Interior lines slider and a live preview. Glass frames, the glass-break overlay and resource-pack priority are handled too.
- **Light Overlay** marks the spots where hostile mobs can spawn (1.8.9 and 1.21.11).
- **Chat history is kept**: in-game chat and your sent messages survive restarts, for each instance. Older pages load as you scroll.
- **Chat emotes**: 64 emotes with shortcodes and a grouped picker. Timestamps take a fixed-width slot and use your real time zone.
- **Arctic Badge styles**: pick a style for your badge. The default is a thin white snowflake. The badge is always shown on 26.2 name tags, and the tab-list icon is square with no shadow.
- **Shoulder pets** (D1).
- **Replays**: open fast on 1.8.9, Forge 1.8.9 works, recording inside a world works, and several replay crashes are fixed.
- **Screenshots** save in the background (F2 no longer stutters), and the viewer opens in one frame.
- **Modrinth previews** for mods, shader packs and resource packs. Packs made for another version are marked incompatible but can still be installed.
- **Feature toggle keys** on every feature page, a padlock to stop the cosmetics preview from rotating, and the version picker remembers its All / Installed / Favorites tab.
- **Title screen**: the snowflake is bigger and slowly spins. Worlds ask before launching.
- **Launcher updates** install quietly in the background.

### Also fixed
- Fog settings work on 1.21.6+ with Sodium, and Fog no longer gets stuck.
- Name tags on 26.2 are sharp up close, Polarium included.
- The crash screen names both mods when two mods contain the same code, and no longer suggests bogus "update X to 1" fixes. It also explains more crashes (clashing mods, failing mixins, silent exits).
- Smoother terrain on 1.8.9 to 1.12.2, a freeze watchdog, cheaper minimap arrows, and faster text rendering.
- Shulker Peek and Inventory Search work in the survival inventory on 1.21.6+.
- A cosmetic or skin that can't be worn says why.
- The window shows on Wayland.
- The access token is kept out of the game's log on 1.8.9 to 1.12.2.
- The Interior lines slider no longer reloads resources while Connected Glass is off.
- Chat lines wrap again after a font switch (smooth font on or off), instead of running past the chat box.
